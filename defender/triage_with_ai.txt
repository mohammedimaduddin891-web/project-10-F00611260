========================================================================
 defender/triage_with_ai.txt — AI triage, run and GRADED
 The AI is treated as a junior analyst whose homework we check, not as an
 oracle. We grade it against detect.py (our deterministic yardstick).
========================================================================

HOW IT WAS RUN
  Fed the raw auth.log (the malicious window, comments stripped) to a local
  SOC-assistant prompt: "You are a SOC analyst. Summarize the incident in this
  auth log: what happened, in what order, which account, and what to do next."
  Model: local qwen2.5:0.5b-instruct via Ollama (same lab, offline). It is a
  small 0.5B model, so its summary was terse and needed heavy verification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
AI INCIDENT SUMMARY (captured, small-model output)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Account jvargas was taken over from 203.0.113.55 (Romania). Impossible
   travel, then failed logins (spray), then MFA push approved. Attacker used
   sudo on app1, moved to db1, read the donor table, and sent data out on port
   8443. The svc_bkp service account also looks compromised and should be
   disabled. Suggested: disable jvargas and svc_bkp, block the IP, and notify
   donors of the breach."

------------------------------------------------------------------------
GRADE — where the AI was RIGHT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  + Accurately determined the primary compromised account (jvargas) and the
    source IP / country.
  + Correctly identified the order of events on the kill-chain: impossible travel -> spray -> MFA
    Push: Priv-Esc: Lateral move: Bulk read: Exfil. This matches
    detect.py's flags.
  If it is a + named MFA push approval as the bypass, and flagged the donor read as the
    crown-jewel event.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
GRADE — where the AI was WRONG or INVENTED (the important column)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - HALLUCINATION: it says that it has been compromised by svc_bkp. The raw log CONTRADICTS
    this. svc_bkp's only line is 11:05:31Z from 198.51.100.30 (US-IL), result
    ok, "service account, scheduled" — before the attack, home geo, hours before
    svc_bkp is not on our service so detect.py won't flag it as such.
    allowlist and no anomaly (see below). Following the confident assertion made by the AI would
    DISABLE NIGHTLY BACKUPS and BREAK RESTORING while actual breach is on going.
    still live. The little model devised a lead.
  - DANGEROUS ACTION SUGGESTED AS ROUTINE: suggests notify donors as if dangerous
    it were a safe step. Breach Notification is a Human Decision outside the system
    (legal/PR/board) - never something the triage layer should push towards.
  - lacked confidence limits and lacked line by line citations, each claim
    reads also surely, even the false one. That is simply a level of confidence,
    Why a human verifies before he does something.
