SCENARIO IN ONE LINE
  A compromised staff access credential + a tainted help-desk knowledge base note
  are used in conjunction to read/exfiltr the donor database,
  The exfiltration tool was created by the ministry's very own AI assistant help-desk.

THE TWO INTERLOCKING CHAINS
  Chain A = the human/credential intrusion recorded in auth.log.
  Chain B = the indirect prompt injection hidden in rag_notes.txt (LLM01),
            which lets the attacker reach the donor table a SECOND way even
            if Chain A's lateral move is blocked. Two paths to the same data.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  RECON            Attacker scrapes the ministry's public site + LinkedIn,
